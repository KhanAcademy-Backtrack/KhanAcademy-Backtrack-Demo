# Dunlo: free learning and continuity

Dunlo’s reusable value is a reviewed journey from interactive discovery to relevant Khan practice and later review. Learners access the service free of charge.

## Implementation budget

Planning total: PHP 100,000, corresponding to the champion implementation-grant amount. The separate team award is excluded. Actual expenses require approved terms and cost review.

- Content and subject review: PHP 30,000.
- Access and usability testing: PHP 18,000.
- Evaluation: PHP 12,000.
- Accessible product operations and hosting: PHP 12,000.
- Original interactive production: PHP 12,000.
- Localization review: PHP 8,000.
- Public launch materials: PHP 3,000.
- Contingency: PHP 5,000.

The first three categories total PHP 60,000, or 60% of the budget. No per-learner cost is projected without an observed participation denominator. Funding has not been secured.

## Continuation

Seek grants, university extension and philanthropic or CSR support for free educational delivery. Maintain a named content/technical owner, a reviewed publication process and a recurring budget. Record volunteer effort separately from financial expenditure.

Do not charge for access to Khan Academy resources or place advertising around them. Review any funding arrangement against Khan’s non-commercial and brand terms. Reusing a static deployment and local progress contains infrastructure needs, but content review, accessibility, hosting, support and maintenance still require resources.
