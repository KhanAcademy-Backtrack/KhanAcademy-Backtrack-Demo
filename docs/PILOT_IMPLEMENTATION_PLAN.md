# Khanpanion: public launch and evaluation plan

Implementation window: November 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027.

Initial access is public and self-service.

## November: prepare and open

Review the discovery content and Khan mappings, test phone and keyboard paths, document maintenance responsibilities, and publish clear support information. Prepare public demonstration material and direct challenge links. Establish a voluntary feedback process and the permissions needed for any structured evaluation.

## December: improve the first journeys

Review feedback about starting, finding relevant Khan work, resuming and understanding explanations. Address access failures and confusing steps. Expand only content that can be reviewed properly. Account for holidays when interpreting return activity.

## January: evaluate continuation

Establish an opt-in evaluation with appropriate permissions. Distinguish exploration, resource opens, learner reports and authorized Khan activity records. Use unfamiliar independent questions and later follow-up where feasible. Report incomplete follow-ups and access barriers.

## February: strengthen reuse

Document how an additional contributor reviews and publishes a content sequence. Review ongoing costs and support effort. Develop further subject mappings or localization only with qualified review. Teacher and community reuse can be explored if access becomes available.

## March: report and continue

Close evaluation in time to review results. Publish reach, engagement and learning findings with their denominators and limits. Report expenditure and volunteer effort, confirm the content/technical maintenance owners, and prepare the next release and support plan.

## Delivery responsibilities

The student team will allocate responsibility for product operations, content and resource review, distribution, evaluation and finance. The adviser supports institutional review. Specific collaborators, learners and institutions are recorded only after agreement.
