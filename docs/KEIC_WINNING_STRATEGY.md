# Dunlo: discovery and continued Khan learning

Current direction: September 13, 2026.

Dunlo makes a useful learning journey begin with curiosity. The public Explore collection connects original playable experiences, focused Khan Academy segments, relevant exercises, authored support and later review. A learner can also enter with a current Khan goal and use the full study companion.

## The case for KEIC

The central mechanism is discover → understand → practise with Khan → return. The feed gives an approachable entrance; the existing recovery and review tools preserve a useful continuation. Khan is visible within the collection and every initial item has a reviewed mapping.

The entry should demonstrate this complete journey with one concrete example, then explain public distribution, content growth, costs and evaluation. Keep audience framing broad. The owner has no access to a recruited learner group, teacher or partner school; the launch therefore starts with direct public access and shared item links.

## What the release demonstrates

- Ten discovery items: six original interactives and four reviewed Khan segments.
- Source-labelled video, opt-in loading, full-lesson links and relevant Khan practice.
- Saved ideas and controls, independent fresh-question handoff, and the existing review tools.
- Direct share links without private answers; phone, keyboard, quiet and reduced-motion paths.
- Existing study packs, quiz preparation, notes, science and co-op retained.

Prepare the public launch, reviewed content and support process in November. Use public demonstrations, searchable topic pages and shareable challenges to introduce the service. School and community adoption can extend the model when opportunities arise. It is not an application prerequisite or an assumed launch dependency. The institution and student fields have already been filled by the owner.
