# Dunlo visual learning release

Built on `e2f03b7`, preserving the chemistry and physics additions alongside the five mathematics areas. No accounts, backend, runtime AI service or new dependency was added.

## Delivered behavior

- Original mathematical workspaces: collecting grouped tiles, signed distribution, four-area factoring with independent sum/product constraints, zero-product number line and graph, repeated roots, equivalent partitions including sums beyond one, mixture scaling and a linked filling/table/graph model.
- Prediction, example-first and independent entrances; step selection, play/pause/replay, numeric controls, static and reduced-motion alternatives. Factor reconstruction asks what would make an earlier pair correct. Exploration is recorded separately from independent work.
- Contextual bookmark pointers, a small wave when called, quiet guidance and a skippable first-visit mascot introduction. The welcome tour spotlights actual intent/navigation controls, dims surrounding content, traps keyboard focus and supports Escape and replay.
- Reviewed routing probes separate sum/product arithmetic, factor/root signs, grouped units, unequal fraction units, proportional scaling and reading coordinates. Probes guide the next support step; they do not pass a skill. There is a bounded investigation and a local help-card exit.
- Version 3 practice adds structural families. Existing versions and per-attempt expressions preserve historical question meaning. Input drafts, original attempts, visual state and reflections remain separate records.
- Guided completion before uncued practice, recall before future-note reveal, truly mixed one-question rehearsal across the selected scope, editable delayed review, and protection against advancing a spaced schedule repeatedly in one sitting.
- Quiz setup accounts for requested topics with supported, reference-only and unsupported entries. Explicit matches and curriculum/version text are learner-confirmed, not claims of full curriculum alignment. Shared scope retains gaps and excludes note cards and private answers.
- Co-op uses Predictor/Explainer roles followed by different individual exit questions. Shared work never enters personal mastery records. Existing personal export/restore keeps its pre-restore backup behavior.

## Manual walkthrough

1. Open Today in a fresh browser. Meet the bookmark; follow or skip the three-pointer tour. It can be replayed from My study space.
2. Open `/demo`, choose 2 and 6, and open the explanation. Predict the matching coefficient, then change the factors to 3 and 4. Inspect the four areas and the two separate constraints.
3. Reconstruct the polynomial that would fit 2 and 6. Then choose a fresh check: the visual disappears and this activity cannot count as independent mastery.
4. Open brackets through Study or a direct route. Collect the same tiles, switch to a signed example and test an incomplete expansion. Pause/reload and confirm the saved step and input.
5. Create a quiz pack with a supported topic and a gap. Confirm the receipt, save, reopen it and share only the scope. Review later using a different problem; attempt recall before revealing a future-self note.

## Boundaries

Offline mini-packs and reviewed Filipino localization are not delivered. The open page may continue during an interruption, but disconnected reload is not promised. Khan media requires a connection. Existing reviewed mathematics resources and focused clip controls are retained; chemistry/physics matches remain explicitly absent until verified. Browser tests validate clip parameters and return behavior, not a new viewing audit of every external lesson.

No automated grading of free-text explanations, no claimed universal mastery, and no claimed learning-outcome advantage over another product. Further content/translation review and consented usability and retention studies remain necessary. [Research-to-implementation decisions](research/2026-09-11/LEARNING_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) explain the evidence and its limits.

Presentation/export work is deferred at the owner’s request; the current scope is the site and documentation only. Deployment outcome is recorded after live verification.
