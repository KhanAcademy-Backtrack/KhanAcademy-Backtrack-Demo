## Learning and motivation

**Retrieval and spacing:** supported learning principles, not an estimate of BACKTRACK’s effect. Sources: IES 2007 practice guide https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/1 and Carpenter, Pan & Butler 2022 https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-022-00089-1 . Safe wording: “The design includes fresh and delayed checks.” Avoid guaranteed retention or a fabricated percentage gain.

**Confidence assessment:** Foster’s trial found no positive attainment difference. It used a different scoring intervention. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-021-10207-9 . Safe wording: “Confidence is routing metadata to test.” Avoid treating it as evidence that BACKTRACK’s routing works.

**Autonomy and competence:** Self-Determination Theory informs choice and visible progress. https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/topics/application-education/ . Safe wording: “Voluntary continuation is a hypothesis.” Avoid “science proves this mechanic is addictive.”

**Teaching at the Right Level:** an important precedent for targeting instruction to demonstrated level; its primary-school context and delivery model differ from this proposal. https://www.povertyactionlab.org/case-study/teaching-right-level-improve-learning . Avoid transferring its outcomes to a secondary mathematics app.

**Philippine remediation context:** DepEd Order 010, s. 2026 concerns the 2026 Summer Remediation Programs. https://www.deped.gov.ph/2026/04/29/april-28-2026-do-010-s-2026-guidelines-for-the-implementation-of-2026-summer-remediation-programs/ . Safe wording: remediation is an active policy concern. Avoid saying prerequisite gaps explain the entire education crisis or that this order approves BACKTRACK.
