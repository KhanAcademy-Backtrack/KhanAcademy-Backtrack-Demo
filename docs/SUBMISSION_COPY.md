## Executive Summary

Word count: 248

Learners need more than another lesson to open. They need to choose a useful next step, understand why it works and remember it when the next problem arrives. Dunlo is a free study companion for Khan Academy that connects those needs without requiring an account.

Learners can start from a supported Khan activity, a study pack, quiz scope or short challenge. They can learn first, practise a familiar idea or work through a confusing step. An adjustable session gives them a manageable starting point. A coverage receipt makes supported topics and remaining gaps visible.

Dunlo’s BACKTRACK engine treats an answer as a clue, then uses a short check to select useful support. Original interactive explanations connect grouped tiles, factor areas, fractions, mixtures and graphs to mathematical symbols. Worked examples and partially completed steps lead into different problems without the guide. Relevant Khan explanations and practice remain available, with the learner’s return point saved.

The reviewer schedules later questions and invites recall before revealing a note. Guided work, fresh independent answers, delayed retrieval and Khan activity remain separate records. A friendly bookmark introduces the interface and offers contextual help that learners can quiet.

The working prototype includes five mathematics areas and introductory chemistry and physics destinations. With UP Manila’s endorsement, we propose an initial usability study followed by a consented November 2026–March 2027 pilot targeting 80–120 learners across 2–3 cohorts. We will assess unfamiliar application, later retention, relevant Khan use, access barriers and teacher effort before claiming learning impact.

## Needs Assessment

Word count: 254

A student preparing for a quiz may have class notes, a topic list and only a short study window. Choosing material does not establish whether the student understands a step or can solve a different problem later. Dunlo addresses that gap between accessing instruction and using it independently.

For x² + 7x + 12 = 0, answers of 2 and 6 could reflect incomplete factor selection, arithmetic trouble or guessing. Answers of 3 and 4 could reflect confusion between factor constants and roots. A short discriminating check helps select support without turning one mistake into an ability judgment.

National evidence motivates attention to learning and access. OECD reports that 16% of assessed Philippine students reached at least Level 2 in mathematics in PISA 2022. PSA reports that 48.8% of households had internet access at home in 2024. These measures describe different populations and do not diagnose Dunlo users or measure their device ownership.

Our initial focus is secondary mathematics learners working on current lessons and quizzes, including new learners, refreshers and confident learners. Teacher-confirmed scope and explicit coverage gaps are more useful than claiming complete curriculum alignment. Optional media, readable phone controls and saved progress address practical constraints, while disconnected reload remains outside the current promise.

Before the larger pilot, we propose walkthroughs with 10–15 learners and discussions with 3–5 teachers, subject to required permissions. We will examine navigation, explanation clarity, unfamiliar questions, language needs, connection failures and preparation time. Findings will determine the first classroom scope and the support that actually needs improvement.

## Business Principles & Financials

Word count: 239

Dunlo keeps learner access free while treating content review, distribution and maintenance as real costs. Its proposed operating model combines reusable learning packs with a teacher routine that fits existing Khan Academy use.

Our PHP 100,000 planning scenario allocates PHP 24,000 to learning design and subject review, PHP 18,000 to access support, PHP 18,000 to evaluation, PHP 16,000 to school implementation, PHP 8,000 to product operations, PHP 8,000 to teacher onboarding and replication, PHP 3,000 to recruitment materials and PHP 5,000 to contingency. These are assumptions pending approved grant terms, actual quotations and an agreed scope.

At the proposed 80–120 enrollees, the scenario represents approximately PHP 833–1,250 per enrollee, including setup. This is not cost per active learner or per successful outcome. We will calculate those measures only after observing participation, outcomes and expenditure, and report staff and volunteer time separately.

Potential university extension, CSR, philanthropic or LGU support could fund implementation and recurring maintenance. These are proposed funding routes, not secured commitments. The team will test who owns the budget, what support educators need and what evidence a funder requires to continue.

The current prototype uses a static site and browser-based saves without an account database or hosted AI tutor. That contains infrastructure complexity but does not eliminate hosting, content maintenance, accessibility testing, translation review, printing or teacher support. Expansion should follow a named maintenance owner, documented recurring costs and evidence that another educator can sustain the routine.

## Innovation

Word count: 260

Dunlo combines original explanatory interactions with a recovery engine that keeps the learner’s goal and return point visible. The innovation is the connected learning experience, rather than a claim that each individual technique is new.

BACKTRACK uses small, authored checks to separate plausible causes of difficulty. For a quadratic attempt of 2 and 6, a sum/product check can distinguish arithmetic trouble from selecting factors using only one condition. An attempt of 3 and 4 leads toward making a single factor zero. These are useful routes to investigate, not automatic diagnoses.

The learner then manipulates mathematical relationships. Tiles regroup without losing their identity. A factoring rectangle links four contributions to the expanded expression, while sum and product remain separate constraints. Fraction partitions preserve the shaded amount. A filling model connects the starting value, rate, table and graph. Static steps, keyboard controls and reduced motion provide alternatives.

A counterfactual challenge asks what would make an earlier answer true. The learner reconstructs a related problem, then faces a different independent question. Worked examples, partial completion and optional explanations provide a bridge instead of requiring unsupported discovery.

The system carries that work into mixed rehearsal and later retrieval. Scope receipts expose unanswered curriculum needs, while the bookmark offers a quiet, contextual pointer. Exploration, reflections, independent answers and resource activity remain distinct.

We adopt established approaches such as retrieval practice, spacing and fading support where they are useful. The proposed pilot will test this combination with unfamiliar assessments and later follow-up. Current engineering tests establish functioning behavior and mathematical checks, not a demonstrated educational advantage.

## Meaningful Khan Academy Integration

Word count: 268

Dunlo connects learners to specific Khan Academy learning goals and preserves a useful route back. Learners can choose a supported lesson, paste a recognized activity URL or open its shared scope. The matching goal shapes the local checks, explanations and practice options.

Mapped mathematics resources appear within packs and sessions with clear labels for Khan Academy explanation and practice. Reviewed video controls retain focused segments, including the factoring clip from 2:22 to 3:56, with replay and optional continuation. Video loads only after learner choice. Original Dunlo visuals and imported source notes remain clearly distinguished from official Khan material.

The intended cycle connects a current goal, useful support, relevant Khan learning or practice, a saved Dunlo return, fresh independent application and later review. A confident learner can move on after demonstrating the step. A new learner can open an explanation first. External activity is a useful option, not a compulsory completion toll.

After Khan practice, a learner can report completion, continued difficulty or an access problem. The response changes suggested support while fresh answers provide separate evidence. Resource opens, learner reports, authorized teacher confirmation and independent work are never treated as interchangeable or automatically synchronized scores.

Teachers can use their existing Khan classes and authorized reports alongside Dunlo’s activity sheets. The prototype does not provide a classroom backend. Chemistry and physics destinations currently lack reviewed Khan matches and say so explicitly, while their original guides remain usable.

The pilot will examine repeated relevant Khan activity across teaching weeks, saved returns, independent application and the teacher time needed to support the cycle. Link counts alone will not establish meaningful integration.

## Potential to Scale

Word count: 268

Dunlo can grow through reusable learning patterns and a small educator routine. Each new scope needs an explicit learning goal, prerequisite rationale, explanations, independent question families, a return schedule and verified resources where available.

The current prototype covers five mathematics areas and four introductory science destinations. It preserves these within Today, Study, Review, My packs and From Khan. Learners can combine supported topics, add a quiz date and keep source-linked note cards. Coverage receipts retain unsupported requests instead of implying that every teacher topic has a complete route.

A shared scope carries topics and coverage information while excluding private answers and notes. Same-screen study alternates Predictor and Explainer roles before different individual exit questions. These mechanisms support reuse without requiring a public forum, account migration or synchronized classroom system.

Replication should begin with one current lesson and an agreed study window. We will measure whether another educator can set up the scope, interpret an activity sheet and maintain the routine without daily intervention from the student team. A handover kit will include resource mappings, assessment definitions, content versions and known access limits.

Phone layouts, optional video, local saves and user-controlled progress export support the present model. Reviewed Filipino explanations and original offline mini-packs remain staged extensions, not delivered capabilities. Additional languages, curriculum versions and science content require competent review and actual use testing.

Growth depends on funded maintenance, subject review, accessibility checks and teacher support. Proposed university, CSR or LGU distribution partnerships require agreement. Our ambition is wider free access across Filipino communities, with expansion guided by observed usefulness and a support model that another institution can sustain.

## Potential Positive Impact

Word count: 261

We aim to learn whether Dunlo helps students use Khan Academy more meaningfully, apply ideas independently and remember them after a delay. These are proposed outcomes, not results already achieved by the working prototype.

The first stage is a consented usability study with new learners, refreshers and confident learners. We will observe whether they can choose a goal, understand the suggested step, request help, return from Khan and resume after an interruption. We will record access failures, confusion, frustration and teacher preparation time before expanding the pilot.

The proposed November 2026–March 2027 pilot targets 80–120 learners across 2–3 cohorts, subject to school access and required permissions. Teachers will review parallel assessment forms. Immediate and 7–14-day follow-up questions will use unfamiliar numbers or representations and separate independent performance from guided practice.

For learners who could not complete the baseline task, retained reentry will require a prespecified success criterion on both fresh immediate and later assessments. The denominator will include all eligible enrolled learners, with missed follow-ups and dropout reported. Already-confident learners will have separate application and retention measures rather than being counted as recovered.

Where practical and approved, a randomized delayed-start comparison will use similar content, study time, access and assessment conditions. A small uncontrolled pre/post gain will not establish causality.

Additional measures will include useful return participation, repeated relevant Khan assignments across teaching weeks, authorized activity evidence, teacher effort and actual costs. Animation completion, confidence, opens and self-reports will remain separate. Success should produce credible evidence and a reusable educator kit, while identifying which learners and conditions require a different approach.
