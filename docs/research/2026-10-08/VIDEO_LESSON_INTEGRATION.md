# Connecting videos to meaningful lesson checks

Research checked 2026-10-08. The owner explicitly requested a research sub-agent in this chat. Competitor comparisons stay in this research record, outside learner-facing content. No questions were copied or adapted, no accounts were used, and this is not an efficacy evaluation.

| Source | What was directly inspected | Design implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TED-Ed temple lesson](https://ed.ted.com/lessons/can-you-solve-the-temple-riddle-dennis-e-shasha/think?lesson_collection=math-in-real-life) | Rendered Watch / Think / Dig Deeper / Discuss flow; Think presents one question at a time with numbered navigation. Saving asks for sign-in. | Stage video, checks and reflection clearly, retaining device-local saves here. |
| [TED-Ed teaching guidance](https://blog.ed.ted.com/2020/09/24/4-free-ted-ed-resources-to-help-support-online-and-in-person-learning/) | Official guidance describes pausing riddles to solve before revealing the solution. | An optional unrevealed prediction, reconsidered at the end, is our recommendation; that exact before-video design was not observed on the lesson page. |
| [H5P Interactive Video](https://h5p.org/interactive-video) | Public demo playback, timeline markers and a question that paused the video. Its documentation describes answer-dependent jumps and bookmarks. | Label verified moments descriptively and offer targeted rewatch after a miss. No H5P dependency is needed. |
| [Edpuzzle choice feedback](https://support.edpuzzle.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007545352-Can-I-include-immediate-feedback-in-the-questions) and [retry guidance](https://support.edpuzzle.com/hc/en-us/articles/12883625699981-Can-I-allow-multiple-attempts) | Both official pages manually opened and read. They describe per-choice feedback, retrying misses and optional rewatching. No authenticated assignment was completed. | Explain the selected distractor's reasoning. Treat repeats as practice, never fresh evidence. |
| [CK-12 lesson features](https://help.ck12.org/hc/en-us/articles/360008241774-FlexBook-2-0-Lesson-Features) | Official overview and lesson-feature documentation read: text, videos, interactives, related material, practice and Next topic together. | Keep relevant support near the question and give a clear next-lesson handoff. These are documented capabilities, not a hands-on learning-outcome result. |
| [OpenStax Physics: Newton's second law](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/4-3-newtons-second-law-of-motion) | Actual section opened. A purpose statement precedes a Khan video, followed by a conceptual check, strategy/worked examples with reveals, practice and definitions. | Recall, explain-why, application and a trap serve different jobs. Write original checks from the actual watched Khan video. |
| [H5P event coverage](https://h5p.org/node/617/xapi-coverage) | Official event documentation manually opened. Interaction, answers and completion are distinct; video completion can describe reaching the end. | Record activity separately from learning evidence. An event name does not establish understanding. |

The pilot design follows prediction → timestamped attention cues → four original checks → selected-choice rationale and targeted support → reflection → optional Khan practice self-report → next lesson. Cues should identify a reasoning move or contrast without revealing the quiz key. A missing segment removes rewatch rather than substituting an unverified range.

The inspected OpenStax Physics page displays CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 wording and TEA attribution requirements. Its expanded citation panel also contains conflicting legacy CC BY 4.0 wording/linking. Record the book's displayed terms and inconsistency if it is later linked; do not assume a common licence across OpenStax books. No Go deeper link is shipped in this pass.

Limits: no pilot Khan captions, new clip ranges, practice pages or video ids were verified by this research. No authenticated competitor assignments or learner outcomes were tested. Khan's mastery article redirected to sign-in and was not hand-verified, so it is not used as a checked source here. Source receipts are in `docs/THIRD_PARTY_MATERIALS.md`.
