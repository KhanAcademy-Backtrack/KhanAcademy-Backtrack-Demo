## Method

Preserve every existing title, order, concept link and save key. Append missing broad reported skills to the appropriate group; new entries have no concept links. Some-source extensions are identified here, and the new Filipino reading group is marked `less`. Retain the 5 October outline as the earlier baseline. A broad title groups related subskills rather than reproducing each provider's wording. Fine single-source extensions are recorded below instead of expanding the outline indefinitely.

Khan matching reuses already reviewed exact-topic videos first. Newly added pages must expose a rendered `(video)` title and the corresponding youtube-nocookie iframe in the in-app browser. No deep URL was guessed or inferred from an empty fetched shell. Full-video playback is paused and optional. Filipino topics are skipped without an English fallback. A searched gap requires at least three distinct Khan searches. Previously reviewed videos keep their original check dates; reuse is not a fresh page verification.

## Exclusions and grouping decisions

The following finer additions were not made into separate topics: scientific notation (A; part of number representation), consumer discounts/markup/profit and work/pipe/age/mixture subcases (A; existing percent, ratio and word problems), absolute value, piecewise graphs, composition, transformations and joint variation (A; existing algebra/functions), restricted counting and conditional probability (A; existing probability/counting), genetic engineering, cladogram subcases and microbiology (A; finer extensions without independent detailed corroboration), Lewis diagrams and named molecular geometries (A; bonding subskills), circular-motion and modern-physics subcases (A; broad motion/energy/radioactivity topics), specific tides/oceanographic subcases (A; bodies of water/astronomy). Introductory history headings in science and Earth's numerical vital statistics (F) do not identify a separate examinable skill. Homonyms (F) belong under existing commonly confused words. English sentence completion/word choice (A) is grouped with context vocabulary and sentence editing; the new Filipino completion title makes the bilingual gap explicit.

Essay-writing advice in F is not corroborated by UP's stated multiple-subtest examination description; it was excluded as a current exam-format claim. Abstract reasoning in U is explicitly a multi-CET chapter and was not added as UPCAT coverage. Existing logic/calculus and astronomy framing remains unchanged. Broad extensions reported only by some sources (for example relativity and practical texts) are reported as such below; they are not predictions of what a future UPCAT will contain.

## Limits

These public reviewer sources do not establish an exhaustive official syllabus, question frequencies, difficulty or a guaranteed future paper. Repeated community checklists may share an original list. Broad course/product headings cannot corroborate individual subskills. Existing titles and concept links were preserved, including carried-forward details whose granular attribution was not renewed. A mapped full video teaches the topic or an explicitly identified component of a broad topic; it does not certify complete coverage of every subskill. Optional companion videos address distinct components without pretending one clip covers everything.

All 173 topics have a decision. There are 139 primary video matches and 34 without a video, including 23 Filipino topics skipped without searching. New rendered-page verification accepted 20 videos; one further page (Reporting measurements) was checked and rejected. Existing catalog resources were reused with their recorded dates; they were not all re-opened in this sweep. No topic remained blocked or not checked. The third-party log lists every primary/companion resource, its recorded date, topic usage and all no-match queries.
