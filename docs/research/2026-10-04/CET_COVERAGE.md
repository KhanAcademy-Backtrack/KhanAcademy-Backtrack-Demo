# Entrance exam subjects, checked 4 October 2026

The reviewer shows only the subjects an exam tests (`src/lib/program/exam-coverage.ts`).
Khanpanion's reviewer has four subjects: Language Proficiency, Reading Comprehension,
Mathematics and Science. When sources disagree, a subject stays in, because leaving out a
tested subject is worse than keeping an extra one.

| Exam | Reviewer subjects | Filipino | Tested but not covered here | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UPCAT | Language, Reading, Math, Science | Yes | None | Official |
| DOST-SEI | Language, Reading, Math, Science | No | Creative reasoning (mechanical, spatial, verbal) | Official |
| DCAT | Language, Reading, Math, Science | No | Mental ability | Not official |
| PUPCET | Language, Reading, Math, Science | Kept | Abstract reasoning | Not official, sources disagree |

## Sources

**UPCAT.** https://upcat.up.edu.ph/htmls/aboutupcat.html says the UPCAT "consists of four
subtests: Language Proficiency (in English and Filipino), Science, Mathematics, and Reading
Comprehension (in English and Filipino)."

**DOST-SEI.** The 2026 Undergraduate Scholarship Examination Primer,
https://science-scholarships.ph/pdf/2026_DOST-SEI_UG_Primer.pdf, sets out three timed parts.
Part I is Creative Reasoning (mechanical, spatial and verbal reasoning) and Language and
Literature ("grammar and syntax, vocabulary, and reading comprehension" in English).
Part II is Science (biology, chemistry, earth science, physics, space science). Part III is
Mathematics. Language and Literature maps to Language Proficiency and Reading Comprehension.
The primer is in English and has no Filipino part.

**DCAT.** DLSU's admissions pages
(https://www.dlsu.edu.ph/admission/undergraduate-admissions/ and the first-year application
page) give dates and procedures but no section list. Public preparation guides consistently
report mental ability, mathematics, science, and English with reading comprehension. None
report a Filipino section. Confirm with the DLSU Admissions and Scholarships Office.

**PUPCET.** PUP's iApply pages (https://www.pup.edu.ph/iapply/pupcet) give schedules and
requirements but no section list. Public guides disagree. Most list English, mathematics,
science and abstract reasoning. Some add Filipino and general information. One says science is
folded into reading passages. Science and Filipino are kept until PUP confirms.

Public preparation guides are third-party review providers. Their names stay out of the
learner-facing site. The site says when a list is not official and links the official page.
