# Entrance exam sections, checked 4 October 2026

The reviewer's exam picker shows each exam's own sections, in the exam's own words
(`src/lib/program/exam-coverage.ts`). Each section maps to the reviewer material that serves
it: Language Proficiency, Reading Comprehension, Mathematics or Science. A section with no
material is still listed, and the reviewer says plainly that it has nothing for it yet.

"Official" means the exam's own published material lists the sections. "Team" means the
Khanpanion team supplied the list. "Reported" means the school publishes no list and public
preparation guides agree on it. The site says when a list is not official and links the
school's page. Review providers' names stay out of the learner-facing site.

## Sources

**UPCAT.** https://upcat.up.edu.ph/htmls/aboutupcat.html says the UPCAT "consists of four
subtests: Language Proficiency (in English and Filipino), Science, Mathematics, and Reading
Comprehension (in English and Filipino)."

**DCAT.** Supplied by the Khanpanion team: Mathematics, Science, English and Mental Ability.
DLSU's admissions pages (https://www.dlsu.edu.ph/admission/undergraduate-admissions/) give
dates and procedures but no section list.

**DOST-SEI.** The 2026 Undergraduate Scholarship Examination Primer,
https://science-scholarships.ph/pdf/2026_DOST-SEI_UG_Primer.pdf, has three timed parts.
Part I (45 minutes) is Creative Reasoning (mechanical, spatial and verbal reasoning) with
Language and Literature ("grammar and syntax, vocabulary, and reading comprehension" in
English). Part II (65 minutes) is Science: biology, chemistry, earth science, physics and space
science. Part III (70 minutes) is Mathematics: algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics and
calculus. There is no Filipino part.

**PUPCET.** Supplied by the Khanpanion team: Mathematics, English, Science, General Information
and Abstract Reasoning. PUP's iApply pages (https://www.pup.edu.ph/iapply/pupcet) give
schedules and requirements but no section list.

**USTET.** UST's admission site, https://ustet.ust.edu.ph/ (FAQ question 17), says: "The USTET
for entry to College covers the three (3) major subject disciplines English, Mathematics, and
Science. In addition, a subtest for Mental Ability completes the entrance test."

**ACET.** Ateneo's ACET page, https://www.ateneo.edu/college/admissions/acet, says the test
"consists of multiple-choice questions in mathematics, English, general knowledge, and abstract
reasoning. It also has an essay segment to test the examinee's writing and reasoning skills."
It lasts about four hours and has no Filipino section. The page blocks automated fetches, so
this wording comes from search results for that page and a guide that quotes it. Open the page
by hand to confirm it.

**PLMAT.** No section list was found on PLM's own site (https://plm.edu.ph/). A Manila Standard
results article and preparation guides describe sub-tests in English, Science, Mathematics,
Filipino and Abstract Reasoning. Some guides leave out Filipino, so it is kept until PLM
confirms.

**TUPSTAT.** TUP's admission requirements
(https://ers.tup.edu.ph/aims/applicants/admissionrequirements.php) name the TUP Scholastic and
Technical Aptitude Test but list no sections. Guides describe mathematics, science, English and
technical reasoning.

**MSU-SASE.** MSU publishes no section list
(https://www.msumain.edu.ph/admissionsoffice/). Guides describe English proficiency,
mathematics, science and mental aptitude.

The DCAT and PUPCET entries replace this file's earlier, lower-confidence lists for those
two exams.
