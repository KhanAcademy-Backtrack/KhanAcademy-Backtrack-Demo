Equations with brackets

Can you solve this?

3(x − 2) = 15

Couldn’t answer? Let’s find the step — not restart the whole subject.

Most people who get stuck here are not missing “algebra”. They are missing one specific rule about what the number outside the bracket is allowed to touch. That takes minutes to repair, once somebody actually finds it.
